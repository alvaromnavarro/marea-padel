A sample day by day

An example, not a fixed schedule

This outline shows one example of how your days could look. Every Marea experience is tailored to your group, so your final schedule may differ depending on court and coach availability, the club calendar, and your group's level and goals. We confirm your day-by-day plan with you before you arrive.

Every training session lasts 1.5 hours and is adapted to your group's level.

  1. Day Day1Welcome and first sessionWelcome pack, meet your BCA Padel coaches and your first training session.
  2. Day Day2Training and a match with local playersYour second training session, then a match against local players of a similar level.
  3. Day Day3Final session and socialA last training session, an organised social with the group, and your photo report and final group video.

Who it's for

Groups who want a brief, intense and well-organised experience, such as a long weekend of padel.

Programmes adapted to all levels: beginner, amateur and professional.

What's included

  • 3 training sessions of 1.5 hours led by BCA Padel
  • 1 match with local players
  • 1 organised social
  • Complimentary welcome pack
  • Photo report
  • Final group video montage
  • Organisation and support throughout the experience

Not included

  • — Flights
  • — Transfers
  • — Accommodation (we're happy to recommend places)
