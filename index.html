Train and enjoy at Krazy Padel

At Marea Padel Experiences we design padel experiences for players, couples and groups who want to combine training, competition, matches with local players and moments of enjoyment on the Costa del Sol.

Activities take place at Krazy Padel, a club in Coín, Málaga, in an ideal setting to train, play and experience padel in a professional, personalised and welcoming way, with training led by the BCA Padel academy.

At Krazy Padel you will enjoy

  • 4 outdoor glass courts
  • Changing rooms
  • Bar and terrace
  • Parking
  • Sports shop
  • Physiotherapy available as an extra
  • Matches, socials and activities with local players

An experience designed for amateur players who want to train with professional quality.

It's not just about training more.
It's about training better.
