Accommodation

Where to stay

Marea doesn't book accommodation, but we're happy to recommend places that suit your group, budget and travel style.

During your padel experience you can stay in different areas of the Costa del Sol, depending on the type of trip, the atmosphere you prefer and the distance to the club.

  • Kira Escapes

    Luxury adults-only wellness retreat and boutique accommodation

  • Dos Iberos Luxury B&B

    Luxury boutique B&B hotel with breathtaking landscapes

  • Villa Lovely Lemon

    Privileged natural setting between Málaga and Marbella

  • Hotel Albaicín

    Restored, modernised 18th-century building in the heart of Coín

Tailored accommodation

We can guide you in choosing hotels, apartments or houses for groups based on budget, location and travel style.

Important

  • — Flights are not included
  • — Transfers are not included
  • — Accommodation is recommended based on group preferences
